fivebyfive

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

fivebyfive is a daily word puzzle you can play on this website and in the iOS app. This page explains what data each one handles. In short: the iOS app only sends what's needed to run fivebyfive Premium subscriptions, and the website uses Google Analytics to understand how the game is played.

iOS app

  • Your games, scores and stats are saved only on your device. They aren't sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple and RevenueCat, a service that manages in-app subscriptions for us. When you open the app, it contacts RevenueCat to load the subscription options and check whether you have Premium. RevenueCat receives:
    • a random ID created by the app, which isn't linked to your name, email or Apple Account,
    • your purchase and subscription history in the app, such as which plan you bought, when, and whether it's active,
    • basic device and app information, such as device model, iOS version, app version, language, App Store country and IP address, and
    • how you interact with the subscription screen, such as opening it, closing it or starting a purchase.
    We use this only to give you access to Premium, restore your purchases, and understand how subscriptions are doing. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for how RevenueCat handles this data.
  • Payments are processed by Apple. We never see your name, payment card or Apple Account details. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • The app has no ads, accounts, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking.

Website

  • Game progress is saved in your browser's local storage on your device. It isn't sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it.
  • Google Analytics records page visits and a few game events: starting a game, placing letters, using undo, finishing a puzzle with your score, and sharing a result. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects standard information such as your browser, device type and approximate location. We use it only to see how the game is played and to improve it. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles this data.
  • Google Fonts provides the site's font, so your browser requests it from Google's servers.

What we don't do

We don't ask for your name, email or any account details. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone beyond the services named on this page, which process it on our behalf.

Deleting your data

To have your subscription data deleted from RevenueCat, email us. Because the app doesn't know who you are, it helps to include a receipt or the date of your purchase so we can find it. Game data on your device is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

fivebyfive isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
