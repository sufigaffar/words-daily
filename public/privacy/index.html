fivebyfive

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

fivebyfive is a daily word puzzle you can play on this website and in the iOS app. This page explains what data each one handles. In short: the iOS app collects nothing, and the website uses Google Analytics to understand how the game is played.

iOS app

  • The app doesn't collect, send or share any data. It has no analytics, ads, accounts or tracking, and works fully offline.
  • Your games, scores and stats are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Website

  • Game progress is saved in your browser's local storage on your device. It isn't sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it.
  • Google Analytics records page visits and a few game events: starting a game, placing letters, using undo, finishing a puzzle with your score, and sharing a result. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects standard information such as your browser, device type and approximate location. We use it only to see how the game is played and to improve it. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles this data.
  • Google Fonts provides the site's font, so your browser requests it from Google's servers.

What we don't do

We don't ask for your name, email or any account details, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Children

fivebyfive isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
